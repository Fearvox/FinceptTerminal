# AutoResearch Program: Fed / FX / EM Opportunities

## Goal
With BMI macro data (3319 .md files, 543 geographies) in vault,
find 3-5 high-edge Manifold markets in these categories:
- Fed rate decisions (use BMI macro-rates)
- FX markets (USD/JPY, EUR/USD, emerging market currencies)
- Emerging market banking crises (BMI risk-banking)
- Election markets with economic drivers (BMI GDP/fiscal)

## Scoring
- Relevance ≥ 7 AND Edge > 10pp
- Liquidity ≥ M$300
- Resolution within 90 days (faster compounding)

## Stop conditions
- 5 iterations OR
- 5 qualified candidates OR
- Diminishing returns (no improvement in 2 iter)

## Iteration strategy
Iter 1: Fed rate decisions (market scan + BMI cross-check)
Iter 2: FX majors (scan USD/JPY/EUR)
Iter 3: EM banking crisis (Argentina, Turkey, Egypt)
Iter 4: Non-US political events (Germany, Brazil, Japan)
Iter 5: Long-tail macro (inflation surprise, GDP miss)
